## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only, no network) writes one original text to `/output/entry.txt`, then reads it back and verifies it is 1–280 characters and not only whitespace, exiting non-zero if the check fails.

## The entry

> Somewhere, a lighthouse keeper lights one lamp at a time. No hurry. The sea keeps its own slow counsel below, and a kettle hums in the warm dark. The night asks nothing of you — and still, you are here, keeping the light.

221 characters. No instruction to relax, no slogan — the steadiness is in the image: one lamp at a time, the sea's slow counsel, the kettle humming, and the quiet fact of still being here, keeping the light.
